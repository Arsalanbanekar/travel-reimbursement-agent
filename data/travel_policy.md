# Sample Corporate Travel Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** FIN-TRV-2025-01  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Applies To:** All full-time employees

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines eligible business travel expenses, reimbursement limits, receipt requirements, and approval rules for employees submitting travel reimbursement claims. The goal is to ensure fair, consistent, and auditable reimbursement while controlling company costs.

## 2. Eligibility

2.1. Only employees who travel for **approved business purposes** are eligible for reimbursement. Personal travel extensions are not reimbursable.

2.2. Travel must be **pre-approved by the employee's direct manager** before departure, except for domestic day trips under **₹25,000** total estimated cost.

2.3. Contractors, interns, and part-time staff are not covered under this policy unless explicitly authorized in writing by Finance.

2.4. Expenses incurred while commuting between home and the primary office are **not** eligible.

## 3. Expense Categories

The following categories may be reimbursed when incurred during approved business travel:

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Flight | Air travel between work locations or to client sites |
| Hotel | Overnight lodging during business travel |
| Meals | Business meals and per-diem meal expenses |
| Taxi / Local Transport | Taxis, rideshare, auto-rickshaw, and local ground transport |
| Parking | Airport or client-site parking fees |
| Internet | In-flight Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot charges for business use |
| Train | Rail travel for business trips (AC Chair Car / 2AC class) |

All claims must itemize expenses by category. Amounts exceeding the limits in Section 4–9 require **Partial Approval** (reimbursement up to the limit only) unless a pre-approved exception exists on file.

## 4. Hotel Policy

4.1. **Domestic hotel limit:** Maximum **₹5,000 per night** (before taxes and fees). Taxes and service charges are reimbursable only if the pre-tax room rate is within the limit.

4.2. **International hotel limit:** Maximum **₹12,000 per night** (or local equivalent converted at the company rate on the date of travel).

4.3. Employees must use **mid-range business hotels**. Luxury properties require pre-approval from a Senior Manager.

4.4. Minibar charges, spa services, and in-room entertainment are **not reimbursable** (see Section 13).

## 5. Flight Policy

5.1. **Domestic flights:** Economy class only. Maximum reimbursable amount: **₹15,000 per one-way or round-trip segment**.

5.2. **International flights:** Economy class only. Maximum reimbursable amount: **₹75,000 per round trip**.

5.3. Upgrades to premium economy, business, or first class are **not reimbursable** without prior written approval from the Finance Head.

5.4. Flight change fees are reimbursable only when the change is required for business reasons and documented on the claim.

## 6. Meal Policy

6.1. **Domestic meals:** Maximum **₹1,000 per day** (total of all meal expenses that day).

6.2. **International meals:** Maximum **₹2,500 per day**.

6.3. Alcohol is reimbursable only when entertaining clients and only if pre-approved; maximum **₹2,500 per person per meal**.

6.4. Meals during commute or non-travel days are not reimbursable.

## 7. Taxi and Local Transport

7.1. **Daily limit:** Maximum **₹2,000 per day** for all taxi, rideshare, and local ground transport combined.

7.2. **Single-trip limit:** No single taxi or rideshare trip may exceed **₹2,500** unless pre-approved.

7.3. Rental cars require pre-approval and are outside the scope of standard taxi reimbursement.

7.4. Tips and platform fees are included within the above limits.

## 8. Parking and Internet

8.1. **Parking:** Maximum **₹500 per day** for airport, hotel, or client-site parking.

8.2. **Internet:** Maximum **₹500 per day** for in-flight Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot charges used for business purposes. Personal streaming or non-work usage is not reimbursable.

## 9. Train Travel

9.1. **Domestic train travel:** Reimbursement for **AC Chair Car or 2AC class** tickets at actual fare, up to a maximum of **₹3,500 per trip**. Sleeper class is not reimbursable for journeys over 4 hours unless pre-approved.

9.2. 1AC or executive class rail tickets are reimbursable only up to the 2AC fare equivalent unless pre-approved.

## 10. Receipt Requirements

10.1. An **itemized receipt** is required for every expense **over ₹500**.

10.2. Each receipt must include: **vendor name**, **date of purchase**, **amount**, and **expense category**.

10.3. Credit card statements or UPI payment screenshots alone are **not sufficient**; an itemized receipt or tax invoice must be attached.

10.4. For expenses ₹500 or below, a receipt is recommended but not mandatory. Claims without receipts for amounts over ₹500 must be routed to **Manual Review** (Section 14).

10.5. Lost receipts require a signed affidavit and manager approval; such claims are always routed to **Manual Review**.

## 11. Submission Timeline

11.1. All reimbursement claims must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the **last day of travel** (travel end date).

11.2. Claims submitted after 30 days will be **automatically rejected** unless an exception is granted by the Finance Head.

11.3. Late submissions due to medical or system outage may be escalated to **Manual Review** with supporting documentation.

## 12. Duplicate Claims

12.1. Each receipt may be submitted **only once**. Submitting the same receipt ID across multiple claims is prohibited.

12.2. If the Duplicate Receipt Checker identifies a receipt already used on a prior claim, the new claim must be routed to **Manual Review**.

12.3. Employees who intentionally submit duplicate receipts may be subject to disciplinary action.

## 13. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are **never reimbursable**, regardless of amount:

- Personal entertainment (concerts, sightseeing tours, spa treatments)
- Traffic fines, parking tickets, and legal penalties
- Personal grooming, laundry (unless trip exceeds 7 days)
- Gifts for clients over **₹4,000** without pre-approval
- Alcohol without client entertainment pre-approval
- Expenses for spouses or family members
- First-class or business-class travel upgrades without approval

Claims consisting primarily of non-reimbursable expenses must be **Rejected**.

## 14. Manual Review Conditions

The following situations require routing to **Manual Review** rather than automatic approval or rejection:

14.1. Missing receipt attachment for any expense over ₹500.

14.2. Missing required fields: vendor, amount, category, or travel date.

14.3. Duplicate receipt ID detected across claims.

14.4. Total claim amount exceeds **₹2,50,000** (requires Finance Head or Executive review).

14.5. International travel without documented manager pre-approval.

14.6. Policy exceptions, conflicting tool results, or ambiguous expense categorization.

14.7. Low-confidence agent decisions where automated rules and policy text conflict.

When in doubt, route to **Manual Review** rather than forcing Approve or Reject.
